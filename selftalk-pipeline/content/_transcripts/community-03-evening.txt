Think for a moment about the people who were with you today — the ones you talked with, the ones you carried something for, the ones who maybe carried something for you. Community is happening around you, even on the ordinary days.

God sets the lonely in families. He is not unaware of the longing for real connection — and He is not passive about it. He places people. He knits lives together. The community you have, and the community still forming around you, is something He is building.

If you reached out today, or let someone in, or admitted something that took courage to say — that was strength. The version of you that can be known by others is more powerful than the version that carries everything alone. You are not diminished by being vulnerable. You are built for it.

Whatever you offered today — a word of encouragement, a listening ear, showing up for someone when it cost you something — it mattered. Each gift used in service to others is not a depletion. It is the thing you were given it for.

You do not have to figure everything out alone tonight. There are people in your corner. There is a God who sees you. You are not isolated — even if the room is quiet right now.

You are not alone. You are known. The people in your life are worth the vulnerability they require. You do not go it alone — and tonight, you rest in that.
