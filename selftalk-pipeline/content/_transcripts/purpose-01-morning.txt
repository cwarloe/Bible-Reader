These are the programs you choose to have... about yourself... and your purpose, your calling, and why you are here.

You are a person of purpose. You were not placed on this earth by accident, and you are not here without a reason. Before you were born, God knew you. He designed you with intention — with specific gifts, a specific place, and a specific contribution that only you can make. You are in charge of showing up to that purpose. That is up to you, and no one else.

When you look at yourself in the mirror, you see someone who is on purpose — not drifting, not wandering, but moving with direction. You see someone who has been given gifts that matter, a voice that is needed, and a calling that is real. You see someone who knows that their daily life is connected to something larger than themselves.

You are God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works which He prepared in advance for you to walk in. Not vague, distant good works — specific ones, placed along your path, designed for you. Your life has been set up with purpose from before you were born. You walk into that, not toward it.

God has plans for you — plans for good and not for harm, plans to give you a future and a hope. That is not a generic promise for all of humanity; it is a specific word over your life. He is actively working all things together for good for you — not some things, all things — because you are called according to His purpose.

You have a calling. You have a contribution. You have a place in this story.

You have been given specific gifts for the common good. Not for show, not to compare, but to serve — to build up the people around you and to advance something that matters. Your gifts are real, they are needed, and they are meant to be used.

Your ordinary days are not interruptions to your calling — they are where it lives. The conversations you have, the work you do, the people you encounter, the integrity you bring to each hour — all of it is the material of a purposeful life. Nothing is wasted in God's economy.

An accurate description of who you are includes the words: called... intentional... gifted... needed... contributing... on mission... making a difference... and living a life that matters.

You are not in your job, your city, your relationships, or your season by accident. God positions people for purposes that are larger than what they can see at the time. You are where you are on purpose, and you steward that well.

Whatever you do, you do it wholeheartedly, as to the Lord and not merely for human approval. That means every task, every meeting, every conversation is infused with meaning. You are not just doing a job — you are serving a purpose, and you know the difference.

You are not here by accident. You are not here without purpose. You are exactly where you are supposed to be.

Every day, you show up to your purpose. Every day, you invest in the calling placed on your life.

You believe in living with direction and intention. The more you show up to who God made you to be, the clearer your purpose becomes. Living on purpose comes naturally, purposefully, and joyfully to you.

You know why you are here. You know that your life matters. You know that the gifts you carry are needed — not someday, but now. And you know that God's purposes for you are not finished until He says they are.

Those are the programs you choose to have about your purpose and calling. You already know the truth: you are here on purpose, gifted for a reason, and called to something that matters... and today is a great day to live that out.
