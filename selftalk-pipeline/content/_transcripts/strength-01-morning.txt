These are the programs you choose to have... about yourself... and your strength, your resilience, and your capacity to keep going.

You are a person who does not quit. Not because you are fearless or effortless, but because you are fueled by something that does not run dry. You can do all things through Christ who strengthens you — and that strength is not dependent on how you feel when you wake up. It is available to you every single day.

When you look at yourself in the mirror, you see someone who has kept going. Someone who has faced hard things and is still here. You see someone who draws on a strength that is not their own, and because of that, is more capable than their circumstances suggest. You are still in the race.

Those who wait on the Lord shall renew their strength. They will mount up with wings as eagles. They will run and not grow weary. They will walk and not faint. That is not a description of someone with better circumstances — it is a description of someone who has learned where strength comes from. That person is you.

When you are weak, then you are strong. That is one of the most counterintuitive truths in scripture, and it is completely reliable. In the moments when you have nothing left of your own, you are in the exact position to access strength that is not your own. Your weakness is not an obstacle to God's power — it is the precondition for it.

You are not done. You are not finished. You have what it takes.

You lay aside every weight and every sin that clings so closely. You do not carry into today what yesterday already resolved. You strip down to what is needed and you run — with endurance, with focus, with your eyes fixed on Jesus who ran the same race and finished it.

You do not grow weary of doing good. In due season, you will reap — if you do not give up. The harvest is coming, and it is tied to your continued showing up. You show up again today, not because it is easy, but because quitting is not who you are.

An accurate description of who you are includes the words: resilient... persistent... fueled from within... not defined by setbacks... able to endure... not a quitter... growing through difficulty... and stronger than your worst day.

His grace is sufficient for you. Whatever you face today — whatever demand, whatever weight, whatever challenge — His grace covers it. You do not have to be enough. You have to show up to the One who is.

You fix your eyes on Jesus, the author and perfecter of your faith, who for the joy set before Him endured the cross. He finished. He endured more than you will ever be asked to endure — and He finished. That is who you are looking to, and that is where your example comes from.

You endure. You press on. You finish what you start.

Every day, you draw on strength that is not your own. Every day, you take one more step forward.

You believe in showing up consistently — even when the results are not visible, even when the progress is slow. Every day you persist, you build something in yourself that cannot be built any other way. Perseverance comes naturally, steadily, and powerfully to you.

You know where your strength comes from. You know that God is not asking you to do this on your own. You know that the strength available to you is greater than any challenge in front of you — and you know that you will finish what you have been given to do.

Those are the programs you choose to have about your strength and perseverance. You already know the truth: you are not too weak, you are not too tired, and you are not done... and today is a great day to prove it.
