Think for a moment about the people in your life — the ones you spoke to today, the ones you thought of, the ones who are woven into your story. You are not isolated. You are not without people. Even on days when that feels hard to believe, it is true.

God sets the lonely in families. He is not unaware of your longing for connection, and He is not passive about it. He places people. He opens doors. He works in the relational fabric of your life in ways you cannot always see in the moment.

If you loved someone today — even imperfectly, even partially — God lives in that. When you love one another, His love is made complete in the world through you. The love you showed today, however small it seemed, carried more weight than you know.

A friend loves at all times. That is what you are practicing — loving at all times, even when it costs something, even when it is not returned perfectly. That consistency is rare and it is valuable and it is who you are becoming.

If something in a relationship stung today — a word, a silence, a disappointment — you can lay it down now. You do not have to process it all tonight. As far as it depends on you, you live at peace. Tonight, it depends on you to rest.

You are not alone. You are loved. The relationships in your life are worth investing in — and you do. Rest in that tonight.
