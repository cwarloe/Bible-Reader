======================================================================
IDENTITY IN CHRIST — EVENING
======================================================================
Suite: identity  |  Track: evening  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Psalm 139:1-18, Romans 8:31-39, Zephaniah 3:17
Target: 5 minutes

  [opening-settle]
  The day is done. Whatever it held — whatever you did well,
  whatever you wish you had done differently — none of it changes
  who you are. You are still chosen. You are still loved. You are
  still His.

  [release-the-day]
  You do not need to carry today into tomorrow. The moments you
  are proud of and the moments you are not — you can release them
  both. God was with you in every one of them, and He remains with
  you now.

  [he-rejoices]
  The Lord your God is with you right now. He is mighty to save.
  He takes great delight in you. He quiets you with His love. He
  rejoices over you. That is the truth about this moment, tonight,
  regardless of how today felt.

  [known-in-the-dark]
  You are known in the quiet and in the dark just as completely as
  in the light. There is nowhere you can go that is beyond His
  presence. Even now, as the day ends, He is here — and He is not
  disappointed in what He sees.

  [tomorrow-is-settled]
  Tomorrow, you will wake up the same person you are tonight —
  chosen, loved, and equipped. Nothing that happened today has
  altered that. You go into sleep secure, and you will wake up the
  same way.

  [closing-rest]
  You are not what you did today.
  You are not what was said about you today.
  You are who God says you are — and that does not change.
  Rest in that tonight.
