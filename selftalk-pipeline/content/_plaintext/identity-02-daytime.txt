======================================================================
IDENTITY IN CHRIST — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: identity  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: Ephesians 1:1-14, 2:1-10, Psalm 139, Romans 8
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  This is who I am.

  [chosen]
  I am chosen by God before the foundation of the world.

  [loved]
  I am deeply and completely loved — not for what I do, but for
  who I am in Christ.

  [redeemed]
  I am redeemed. My past is paid for. I walk in the new.

  [adopted]
  I am an adopted child of God. I belong to Him fully and
  permanently.

  [sealed]
  I am sealed with the Holy Spirit. My identity in God is secure
  and cannot be taken from me.

  [known]
  God knows me completely and loves me completely. I am not
  hidden, and I am not a disappointment.

  [no-condemnation]
  There is no condemnation for me. The accuser has no standing. I
  am accepted.

  [his-workmanship]
  I am God's workmanship, created for purposes He prepared for me
  in advance.

  [not-defined-by-past]
  I am not defined by my failures or my past. I am defined by what
  God says about me.

  [nothing-separates]
  Nothing separates me from the love of God — not my worst day,
  not my longest struggle.

  [seated]
  I am seated with Christ. I live from that position, not toward
  it.

  [fearfully-made]
  I am fearfully and wonderfully made. That truth does not change
  with my circumstances.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words and let them settle, I strengthen
  my sense of who I truly am. I am who God says I am — and that is
  exactly how I choose to live.

  [closing-anchor]
  This is who I am.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SYNTHESIS (second-person coda):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  This is who you are.
  You are chosen by God before the foundation of the world. You
  are deeply and completely loved — not for what you do, but for
  who you are in Christ. You are redeemed. Your past is paid for.
  You walk in the new.
  You are adopted into God's family, sealed with His Spirit, and
  known completely by the One who made you. Not hidden. Not a
  disappointment. Known and loved.
  There is no condemnation for you. None. The accuser has no
  standing over your life. You are accepted, and that verdict does
  not change.
  You are God's workmanship — created with intention, placed with
  purpose, and equipped for everything set before you.
  Nothing separates you from the love of God. Not your worst day.
  Not your longest struggle. Not anything.
  You are seated with Christ. You live from that position. You do
  not strive toward it — you already have it.
  Each time you hear these words and let them settle, you
  strengthen your sense of who you truly are. You are who God says
  you are — and that is exactly how you choose to live.
  This is who you are.
