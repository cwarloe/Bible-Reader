======================================================================
SPIRITUAL AUTHORITY — EVENING
======================================================================
Suite: authority  |  Track: evening  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Psalm 91:1-4, 11-12, Ephesians 6:13, 1 Peter 5:8-9
Target: 5 minutes

  [opening-settle]
  The day is done, and you are still standing. Whatever came at
  you today — accusation, discouragement, temptation, doubt — you
  made it through. You stood your ground. That counts.

  [dwell-in-shelter]
  You dwell in the shelter of the Most High. You rest in the
  shadow of the Almighty. Those are not poetic words — they are
  your actual location tonight. You are covered. The armor does
  not come off when you close your eyes; the One who guards you
  does not sleep.

  [angels-assigned]
  He has given His angels charge over you to guard you in all your
  ways. You are not unprotected in the night. What you cannot see
  is not absent. You are watched over by what you cannot detect
  and guarded by what you cannot quantify.

  [having-done-all-stand]
  Having done all that the day required, you stand. You did not
  have to win every battle perfectly today — you only had to stay
  on the field. And you did. That is what standing firm looks like
  in real life, and you did it.

  [he-handles-the-night]
  The enemy prowls — but you are not defenseless, and you are not
  alone. Be alert, be sober, resist — and then rest, because the
  One who called you to stand is also the One who watches while
  you sleep.

  [closing-rest]
  You are still standing.
  You are covered for the night.
  The victory is not yours to maintain while you sleep — it is His
  to hold.
  Rest in that.
