======================================================================
GRACE & FORGIVENESS — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: grace  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: Ephesians 2:1-10, Romans 5:1-11, 8:1, 1 John 1:9, Psalm 103
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  I am fully forgiven and walking in grace.

  [forgiven]
  I am fully forgiven — not partially, not provisionally. My sins
  are gone as far as the east is from the west.

  [justified]
  I am justified by faith. I am declared righteous before God —
  not by my performance, but by grace.

  [no-condemnation]
  There is no condemnation for me. None. I am free from the weight
  of guilt and shame.

  [not-on-probation]
  I am not on probation with God. I am not earning my way back. I
  am already accepted.

  [saved-by-grace]
  I am saved by grace through faith — not by works. This is the
  gift of God. I cannot add to it.

  [peace-with-god]
  I have peace with God through Jesus Christ. That peace is
  settled and it does not depend on my feelings.

  [access]
  I have direct access to God — right now, as I am. I approach Him
  with confidence, not with shame.

  [compassion]
  God is compassionate and gracious toward me. He knows how I am
  formed. He is for me, not against me.

  [no-record]
  God does not keep a record of my wrongs. He does not hold my
  failures over me. They are gone.

  [grace-motivates]
  Grace is not an excuse — it is the power to become who I was
  created to be. I live well because I am already loved.

  [it-is-finished]
  It is finished. The price has been paid. Nothing I do today can
  add to or take away from what Christ accomplished.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words and let them settle, I replace the
  lie that I have to earn my place. I am accepted. I am free. I
  walk in grace.

  [closing-anchor]
  I am fully forgiven and walking in grace.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SYNTHESIS (second-person coda):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  You are fully forgiven and walking in grace.
  You are justified by faith — declared righteous, not by your
  performance, but by grace. Your sins are gone as far as the east
  is from the west. God does not hold them over you. He does not
  keep a record.
  There is no condemnation for you. None. You are not on
  probation. You are not earning your way back. You are already
  accepted.
  You have been saved by grace through faith — not by works. It is
  the gift of God, and you cannot add to it or lose it through
  your performance.
  You have peace with God. You have direct access to Him — right
  now, as you are. You approach Him with confidence, not with
  shame.
  God is compassionate and gracious toward you. He knows how you
  are formed. He is for you.
  Grace is not an excuse. It is the power to become who you were
  created to be. You live well because you are already loved.
  It is finished. The price has been paid. Nothing you do today
  can add to or subtract from what Christ has already accomplished
  on your behalf.
  Each time you hear these words and let them settle, you replace
  the lie that you have to earn your place. You are accepted. You
  are free. You walk in grace.
  You are fully forgiven and walking in grace.
