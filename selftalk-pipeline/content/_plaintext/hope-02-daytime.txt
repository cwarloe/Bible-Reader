======================================================================
HOPE & REDEMPTION — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: hope  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: Joel 2:25, Romans 8:28, Philippians 1:6, Lamentations 3:21-23, Isaiah 43:18-19, 1 Peter 1:3-4
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  My story is not over. God is still working in my life.

  [not-too-late]
  I am not too late. The window has not closed. God is not limited
  by my timeline or my history.

  [mercies-new]
  His mercies are new every morning. I begin today with fresh
  access to the compassion and faithfulness of God.

  [he-restores]
  God restores what was lost. He restores the years. What looks
  like subtraction to me, He is able to multiply.

  [new-thing]
  God is doing a new thing — even now it springs up. I do not
  dwell only on former things. I watch for what He is building.

  [he-completes]
  He who began a good work in me will carry it to completion. That
  work did not expire. He is faithful to finish what He started.

  [all-things]
  God works all things together for good. All things — including
  the losses, the detours, and the seasons I count as wasted.

  [living-hope]
  I have a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ —
  a hope that does not disappoint and does not run out.

  [not-counted-out]
  I am not counted out. I am not too old. I am not too far gone.
  God still has a purpose for the next faithful step I take.

  [not-wasted]
  Nothing done faithfully is wasted. Every honest step, every act
  of faithfulness today builds something that accumulates and
  lasts.

  [redemptive-arc]
  God works redemptively in broken timelines. That is His
  specialty — and my story is one He is actively redeeming.

  [today-matters]
  Today matters. Today's faithfulness is not nothing. It is the
  beginning of something God is building in me and through me.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words, I reinforce the truth: I am not
  too late, my story is not over, and God is still at work in my
  life.

  [closing-anchor]
  My story is not over. God is still working in my life.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SYNTHESIS (second-person coda):

  Your story is not over. God is still working in your life.
