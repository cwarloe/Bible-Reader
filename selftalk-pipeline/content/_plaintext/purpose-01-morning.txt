======================================================================
PURPOSE & CALLING — MORNING
======================================================================
Suite: purpose  |  Track: morning  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Ephesians 2:10, Jeremiah 29:11, Romans 8:28-30, 1 Corinthians 12:4-7, Colossians 3:23-24
Target: 8 minutes

  [opening-frame]
  These are the programs you choose to have... about yourself...
  and your purpose, your calling, and why you are here.

  [recognition]
  You are a person of purpose. You were not placed on this earth
  by accident, and you are not here without a reason. Before you
  were born, God knew you. He designed you with intention — with
  specific gifts, a specific place, and a specific contribution
  that only you can make. You are in charge of showing up to that
  purpose. That is up to you, and no one else.

  [mirror-check]
  When you look at yourself in the mirror, you see someone who is
  on purpose — not drifting, not wandering, but moving with
  direction. You see someone who has been given gifts that matter,
  a voice that is needed, and a calling that is real. You see
  someone who knows that their daily life is connected to
  something larger than themselves.

  [workmanship]
  You are God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good
  works which He prepared in advance for you to walk in. Not
  vague, distant good works — specific ones, placed along your
  path, designed for you. Your life has been set up with purpose
  from before you were born. You walk into that, not toward it.

  [plans-for-you]
  God has plans for you — plans for good and not for harm, plans
  to give you a future and a hope. That is not a generic promise
  for all of humanity; it is a specific word over your life. He is
  actively working all things together for good for you — not some
  things, all things — because you are called according to His
  purpose.

  [tricolon-purpose]
  You have a calling.
  You have a contribution.
  You have a place in this story.

  [gifted-for-the-common-good]
  You have been given specific gifts for the common good. Not for
  show, not to compare, but to serve — to build up the people
  around you and to advance something that matters. Your gifts are
  real, they are needed, and they are meant to be used.

  [every-day-counts]
  Your ordinary days are not interruptions to your calling — they
  are where it lives. The conversations you have, the work you do,
  the people you encounter, the integrity you bring to each hour —
  all of it is the material of a purposeful life. Nothing is
  wasted in God's economy.

  [descriptor-series]
  An accurate description of who you are includes the words:
  called... intentional... gifted... needed... contributing... on
  mission... making a difference... and living a life that
  matters.

  [not-by-accident]
  You are not in your job, your city, your relationships, or your
  season by accident. God positions people for purposes that are
  larger than what they can see at the time. You are where you are
  on purpose, and you steward that well.

  [work-as-to-the-lord]
  Whatever you do, you do it wholeheartedly, as to the Lord and
  not merely for human approval. That means every task, every
  meeting, every conversation is infused with meaning. You are not
  just doing a job — you are serving a purpose, and you know the
  difference.

  [tricolon-called]
  You are not here by accident.
  You are not here without purpose.
  You are exactly where you are supposed to be.

  [every-day]
  Every day, you show up to your purpose.
  Every day, you invest in the calling placed on your life.

  [investment]
  You believe in living with direction and intention. The more you
  show up to who God made you to be, the clearer your purpose
  becomes. Living on purpose comes naturally, purposefully, and
  joyfully to you.

  [knowing]
  You know why you are here. You know that your life matters. You
  know that the gifts you carry are needed — not someday, but now.
  And you know that God's purposes for you are not finished until
  He says they are.

  [closing-anchor]
  Those are the programs you choose to have about your purpose and
  calling. You already know the truth: you are here on purpose,
  gifted for a reason, and called to something that matters... and
  today is a great day to live that out.
