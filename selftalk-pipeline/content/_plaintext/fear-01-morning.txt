======================================================================
FEAR & ANXIETY — MORNING
======================================================================
Suite: fear  |  Track: morning  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Philippians 4:6-7, Isaiah 41:10, 43:1-3, Psalm 23, 1 John 4:18, Matthew 6:25-34
Target: 8 minutes

  [opening-frame]
  These are the programs you choose to have... about yourself...
  and about fear, anxiety, and the peace that is already yours.

  [recognition]
  You are a person who lives in peace — not because your
  circumstances are safe, but because the One who holds all things
  is on your side. You are not ruled by fear. Anxiety does not
  have the final word in your life. You are in charge of where you
  focus your mind, and what you choose to trust. That is up to
  you, and no one else.

  [mirror-check]
  When you look at yourself in the mirror, you see someone who is
  settled, grounded, and at peace. You see someone who faces
  uncertainty without flinching — not because you are fearless,
  but because you know who goes before you. You are a person who
  brings your worries to God and receives His peace in exchange.

  [do-not-fear]
  God says to you: do not fear, for I am with you. Do not be
  anxious, for I am your God. I will strengthen you. I will help
  you. I will uphold you with my righteous right hand. That is a
  direct promise to you — not a general statement, but a specific
  word spoken over your life.

  [called-by-name]
  You are called by name. When you pass through deep water, He is
  with you. When you pass through fire, you are not burned. The
  God who holds the universe holds you — personally, specifically,
  by name.

  [tricolon-peace]
  You are not anxious.
  You are not overwhelmed.
  You are held.

  [present-not-future]
  You do not borrow trouble from tomorrow. Each day has enough of
  its own, and you meet each one with what you need for that day —
  no more and no less. You do not live in a future that has not
  arrived. You are present, grounded, and trusting.

  [peace-beyond-understanding]
  You bring everything to God — every worry, every uncertainty,
  every thing that keeps trying to steal your attention — through
  prayer and petition, with thanksgiving. And the peace of God,
  which surpasses all understanding, guards your heart and your
  mind. That is not a wish. That is what happens when you pray.

  [descriptor-series]
  An accurate description of who you are includes the words:
  grounded... settled... trusting... present... steady...
  prayerful... at peace... not controlled by worst-case
  thinking... and confident in the One who holds your future.

  [perfect-love]
  Perfect love drives out fear. You are loved with a perfect love
  — and that love is greater than any threat, any uncertainty, or
  any outcome you are afraid of. You do not have to be afraid. The
  love of God is the antidote to every fear that comes at you.

  [what-to-think-about]
  You train your mind to dwell on what is true, what is honorable,
  what is just, what is pure, what is lovely, what is commendable.
  You do not feed your anxiety by rehearsing your fears. You feed
  your peace by focusing on what God says is real.

  [tricolon-trust]
  You trust Him with today.
  You trust Him with tomorrow.
  You trust Him with all of it.

  [every-day]
  Every day, you choose peace over anxiety.
  Every day, you bring your worries to God and receive His peace
  in return.

  [investment]
  You believe in building a settled, trusting mind. Every time you
  return to these truths, you reinforce the neural pathways of
  peace over panic. Living in God's peace comes more naturally to
  you every day.

  [knowing]
  You know that He is with you. You know that He goes before you.
  You know that He holds what you cannot control — and you know
  that nothing catches Him by surprise.

  [closing-anchor]
  Those are the programs you choose to have about fear and
  anxiety. You already know the truth: you are not ruled by fear.
  You are grounded in God's peace... and today is a great day to
  live that way.
