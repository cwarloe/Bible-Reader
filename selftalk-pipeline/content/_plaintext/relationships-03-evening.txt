======================================================================
RELATIONSHIPS & LOVE — EVENING
======================================================================
Suite: relationships  |  Track: evening  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: 1 John 4:11-12, Romans 12:18, Proverbs 17:17, Psalm 68:6
Target: 5 minutes

  [opening-settle]
  Think for a moment about the people in your life — the ones you
  spoke to today, the ones you thought of, the ones who are woven
  into your story. You are not isolated. You are not without
  people. Even on days when that feels hard to believe, it is
  true.

  [god-sets-lonely-in-families]
  God sets the lonely in families. He is not unaware of your
  longing for connection, and He is not passive about it. He
  places people. He opens doors. He works in the relational fabric
  of your life in ways you cannot always see in the moment.

  [if-we-love-one-another]
  If you loved someone today — even imperfectly, even partially —
  God lives in that. When you love one another, His love is made
  complete in the world through you. The love you showed today,
  however small it seemed, carried more weight than you know.

  [a-friend-loves-always]
  A friend loves at all times. That is what you are practicing —
  loving at all times, even when it costs something, even when it
  is not returned perfectly. That consistency is rare and it is
  valuable and it is who you are becoming.

  [release-what-stung]
  If something in a relationship stung today — a word, a silence,
  a disappointment — you can lay it down now. You do not have to
  process it all tonight. As far as it depends on you, you live at
  peace. Tonight, it depends on you to rest.

  [closing-rest]
  You are not alone.
  You are loved.
  The relationships in your life are worth investing in — and you
  do.
  Rest in that tonight.
