======================================================================
STRENGTH & PERSEVERANCE — EVENING
======================================================================
Suite: strength  |  Track: evening  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Isaiah 40:29-31, Psalm 73:26, Lamentations 3:22-23, 2 Corinthians 4:16-17
Target: 5 minutes

  [opening-settle]
  You made it through today. Whatever it cost you — whatever it
  took to keep showing up — you are still here, and that is not
  nothing. In fact, that is a great deal.

  [outwardly-but-inwardly]
  Even if your outer self feels worn down tonight, your inner self
  is being renewed day by day. That is not wishful thinking — that
  is what God is doing in the unseen work of perseverance. The
  hard days are forming something in you that the easy days
  cannot.

  [light-momentary]
  These light and momentary troubles are achieving for you an
  eternal weight of glory that far outweighs them all. Not all
  trouble feels light and momentary when you are in it — but the
  perspective is real, and it is worth holding on to tonight.

  [strength-for-tomorrow]
  He gives power to the faint, and to those who have no might He
  increases strength. The strength you will need tomorrow is not
  something you have to manufacture tonight. He gives it. You
  rest, and He replenishes.

  [not-alone-in-it]
  My flesh and my heart may fail — but God is the strength of my
  heart and my portion forever. You do not have to be strong
  enough tonight. He is what you need, and He is not running out.

  [closing-rest]
  You did not quit today.
  That matters more than you know.
  Rest now. He restores.
  Tomorrow, you rise again.
