======================================================================
STRENGTH & PERSEVERANCE — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: strength  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: Philippians 4:13, Isaiah 40:31, Hebrews 12:1-3, 2 Corinthians 12:9, Galatians 6:9
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  I am strong. I do not quit.

  [can-do-all-things]
  I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me. That
  strength is available to me right now.

  [renewed]
  I wait on the Lord and He renews my strength. I mount up. I run
  and do not grow weary. I walk and do not faint.

  [weak-then-strong]
  When I am weak, then I am strong. My weakness is not an obstacle
  — it is where God's power shows up.

  [grace-sufficient]
  His grace is sufficient for me. I do not have to be enough on my
  own. I show up to the One who is.

  [not-done]
  I am not done. I am not finished. I have what it takes — not
  because of me, but because of who is in me.

  [lay-aside]
  I lay aside every weight that slows me down. I do not carry
  yesterday into today. I run free.

  [do-not-give-up]
  I do not grow weary of doing good. In due season I will reap —
  if I do not give up. So I do not give up.

  [fixed-on-jesus]
  I fix my eyes on Jesus — who endured more than I will ever face
  and finished. That is my model and my strength.

  [not-defined-by-setbacks]
  I am not defined by my setbacks, my failures, or my worst days.
  I am defined by the One who is renewing me.

  [press-on]
  I press on toward what is ahead. I do not dwell on what is
  behind me. Forward is the direction I move.

  [consistent]
  I show up consistently — even when results are not visible, even
  when progress is slow. Every step builds something real.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words, I reinforce my capacity to endure.
  I draw on strength that is not my own, and it is more than
  enough.

  [closing-anchor]
  I am strong. I do not quit.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SYNTHESIS (second-person coda):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  You are strong. You do not quit.
  You can do all things through Christ who strengthens you. That
  strength is available to you right now — not when you feel
  better, not when circumstances improve. Right now.
  Those who wait on the Lord renew their strength. You mount up.
  You run and do not grow weary. You walk and do not faint.
  When you are weak, you are strong. Your weakness is not an
  obstacle — it is where God's power shows up most clearly.
  His grace is sufficient for you. You do not have to be enough on
  your own. You show up to the One who is.
  You are not done. You are not finished. You lay aside every
  weight and you run — free, focused, forward.
  You do not grow weary of doing good. In due season you will reap
  — if you do not give up. So you do not give up.
  You fix your eyes on Jesus — who endured more than you will ever
  face and finished. That is your model and your strength.
  You are not defined by your setbacks or your failures. You press
  on toward what is ahead.
  Each time you hear these words, you reinforce your capacity to
  endure. You draw on strength that is not your own — and it is
  more than enough.
  You are strong. You do not quit.
