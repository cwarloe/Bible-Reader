======================================================================
WORK AS HOLY CALLING — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: work  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: Ephesians 6:5-9, Colossians 3:23-24, Genesis 1:28, Matthew 25:21
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  My work is holy. I am on mission today.

  [not-a-layman]
  I am not just a layman doing secular work. I am one of the
  saints through whom the wisdom of God is made known.

  [mission-field]
  My workplace is my mission field. I go to work as a person on
  assignment from God.

  [as-to-the-lord]
  Whatever I do, I do it wholeheartedly — as to the Lord and not
  for human approval. I work for an audience of One.

  [holy-calling]
  My daily work is a holy calling. When I steward my work with
  excellence, I carry out God's original design for humanity.

  [formed-by-hard]
  I welcome the hardships that come through my work as instruments
  God uses to form Christ in me.

  [servant-at-work]
  I serve the people around me at work — with integrity, genuine
  care, and the presence of someone who carries something they
  need.

  [not-paycheck]
  My work is not just a paycheck. I hold what I earn with an open
  hand, converting it into purposes that outlast my career.

  [not-my-god]
  My work is not my god. I work hard and I rest well. I know how
  to hold both.

  [ultimate-employer]
  God is my ultimate employer. He sees my sincerity, my integrity,
  and the quality of what I bring — every day.

  [full-time-service]
  I am in full-time service to God. My ministry does not start on
  Sunday — it runs all week, wherever I am placed.

  [well-done]
  I work faithfully toward the day I hear: Well done. You were
  faithful with a few things. Enter into the joy of your Master.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words, I reinforce the truth that my work
  matters eternally. I show up today as someone on assignment from
  God.

  [closing-anchor]
  My work is holy. I am on mission today.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SYNTHESIS (second-person coda):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Your work is holy. You are on mission today.
  You are not just a layman doing secular work. You are one of the
  saints through whom the wisdom of God is made known. Your
  workplace is your mission field, and you go to it as a person on
  assignment from God.
  Whatever you do, you do it wholeheartedly — as to the Lord and
  not for human approval. You work for an audience of One. Your
  performance review is not the final word. God sees your
  sincerity, your integrity, and the quality of what you bring.
  Your daily work is a holy calling. When you steward it with
  excellence, you carry out God's original design. You welcome the
  hard seasons as instruments God uses to form Christ in you.
  You serve the people around you — with integrity, genuine care,
  and the presence of someone who carries something they cannot
  name but can feel.
  Your work is not your god. You hold what you earn with an open
  hand, converting it into purposes that outlast your career.
  You are in full-time service to God. Your ministry runs all
  week, wherever you are placed.
  You work faithfully toward the day you hear: Well done. You were
  faithful with a few things. Enter into the joy of your Master.
  Each time you hear these words, you reinforce the truth that
  your work matters eternally. You show up today as someone on
  assignment from God.
  Your work is holy. You are on mission today.
