======================================================================
FEAR & ANXIETY — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: fear  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: Philippians 4:6-7, Isaiah 41:10, Psalm 23, 1 John 4:18, Matthew 6:34
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  I live in peace. I am not ruled by fear.

  [not-anxious]
  I am not anxious. I bring my worries to God and receive His
  peace in return.

  [he-is-with-me]
  God is with me. He strengthens me, He helps me, He upholds me. I
  do not face anything alone.

  [do-not-fear]
  I do not fear the future. God holds what I cannot see and He is
  not surprised by anything coming my way.

  [called-by-name]
  I am called by name. When I pass through deep water, He is with
  me. I am not consumed.

  [peace-beyond-understanding]
  The peace of God, which surpasses understanding, guards my heart
  and mind. That is what happens when I pray.

  [present]
  I live today — not in tomorrow's fears. I do not borrow trouble.
  Each day brings what I need for that day.

  [perfect-love]
  I am loved with a perfect love, and perfect love drives out
  fear. I do not have to be afraid.

  [what-i-think-about]
  I choose what I dwell on. I fix my mind on what is true,
  honorable, and good — not on worst-case thinking.

  [he-goes-before]
  He goes before me into every situation I will face today. I walk
  into nothing unprepared and nothing alone.

  [trust]
  I trust Him with today, with tomorrow, and with everything I
  cannot control.

  [settled]
  I am settled in my spirit. Anxiety does not get to drive. Peace
  is my natural state.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words, I reinforce the choice to live in
  peace. I am not ruled by fear. I am grounded in God.

  [closing-anchor]
  I live in peace. I am not ruled by fear.
