======================================================================
GRACE & FORGIVENESS — MORNING
======================================================================
Suite: grace  |  Track: morning  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Ephesians 2:1-10, Romans 5:1-11, 8:1-4, 1 John 1:9, Psalm 103:8-14
Target: 8 minutes

  [opening-frame]
  These are the programs you choose to have... about yourself...
  and the grace and forgiveness that are already yours in Christ.

  [recognition]
  You are a person who has been lavished with grace — unearned,
  undeserved, and unconditional. Not because you performed well
  enough or tried hard enough, but because God, who is rich in
  mercy, made you alive together with Christ. That is your
  standing. It does not fluctuate with your behavior.

  [mirror-check]
  When you look at yourself in the mirror, you see someone who has
  been fully forgiven. Not partially. Not provisionally. Fully.
  You see someone who has been justified by faith — declared
  righteous, not by achievement, but by grace through faith. That
  is the person you are looking at.

  [no-performance-required]
  You are not on probation with God. You are not in a season of
  earning your way back. You have been saved by grace through
  faith — and that is not of yourself. It is the gift of God. You
  cannot add to it and you cannot take away from it by your
  performance.

  [far-as-east-from-west]
  God has removed your sins as far as the east is from the west.
  He does not hold them over you. He does not bring them up. He
  does not keep a record of wrongs. The same God who knows every
  failure you have ever had has chosen not to count any of them
  against you.

  [tricolon-grace]
  You are forgiven.
  You are justified.
  You are at peace with God.

  [no-condemnation]
  There is no condemnation for you — none. The law of the Spirit
  of life has set you free. You are not under the weight of guilt
  that the enemy wants you to carry. You have been set free, and
  that freedom is real and it is complete.

  [access-to-god]
  Because of what Christ has done, you have direct access to God.
  Not when you get it together. Not after you have proven
  yourself. Right now, as you are, you have full access. You can
  approach His throne with confidence, not with shame.

  [descriptor-series]
  An accurate description of who you are includes the words:
  forgiven... justified... reconciled... accepted... covered...
  clean... free... at peace with God... and walking in grace.

  [grace-not-license]
  You understand that grace is not an excuse to remain the same —
  it is the power to become who you were created to be. Grace
  motivates you. It does not paralyze you. You live well not to
  earn God's favor, but because you already have it.

  [compassion-and-mercy]
  The Lord is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger and
  abounding in love. He knows how you are formed. He remembers
  that you are human. His mercy toward you is as high as the
  heavens are above the earth. He is for you, not against you.

  [tricolon-standing]
  You do not earn His love.
  You do not maintain His forgiveness.
  You rest in it.

  [every-day]
  Every day, you receive grace — not because you deserve it, but
  because He gives it.
  Every day, you walk in the freedom of full forgiveness.

  [investment]
  You believe in returning to this truth every morning. The more
  you hear it, the more it replaces the lie that you have to earn
  your place. Living in grace comes naturally, freely, and
  gratefully to you.

  [knowing]
  You know what was done for you. You know the price that was
  paid. You know that it is finished. And you know that nothing
  you do today can add to or subtract from what Christ has already
  accomplished on your behalf.

  [closing-anchor]
  Those are the programs you choose to have about grace and
  forgiveness. You already know the truth: you are fully forgiven,
  completely accepted, and walking in grace that you did not earn
  and cannot lose... and today is a great day to live like it.
