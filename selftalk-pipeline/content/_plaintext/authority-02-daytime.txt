======================================================================
SPIRITUAL AUTHORITY — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: authority  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: Ephesians 6:10-18, Luke 10:19, Colossians 2:15, James 4:7, Romans 8:37, 1 John 4:4
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  I am fully equipped and standing in the authority of Christ.

  [strong-in-the-lord]
  I am strong in the Lord and in the strength of His mighty power.
  Not my own — His.

  [enemy-disarmed]
  The enemy I face is a defeated enemy. At the cross, God disarmed
  him and triumphed over him publicly.

  [authority-given]
  I have been given authority over all the power of the enemy.
  Nothing will harm me.

  [greater-in-me]
  Greater is He who is in me than he who is in the world. I do not
  operate from fear — I operate from that truth.

  [resist]
  I submit to God and I resist the enemy. He flees. That is the
  promise and it works.

  [full-armor]
  I take up the full armor of God. I am not going into this day
  unprotected or unprepared.

  [truth-shield]
  I stand on truth, I am covered by righteousness, and I carry the
  shield of faith that stops every lie sent my way.

  [not-a-victim]
  I am not a victim of spiritual attack. I am a victor in Christ.
  That is my standing and I walk in it.

  [more-than-conqueror]
  In all things, I am more than a conqueror through Him who loved
  me. Not surviving — conquering.

  [prayer-first]
  I pray at all times in the Spirit. Prayer is my first move, not
  my last resort.

  [no-ground-given]
  I give no ground to the enemy — no foothold, no room, no access.
  I bring every lie into the light of truth.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words, I reinforce my standing in Christ.
  I am not fighting for victory — I am fighting from it.

  [closing-anchor]
  I am fully equipped and standing in the authority of Christ.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
