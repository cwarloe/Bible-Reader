======================================================================
IDENTITY IN CHRIST — MORNING
======================================================================
Suite: identity  |  Track: morning  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Ephesians 1:1-14, 2:1-10, Psalm 139:1-18, Romans 8:14-17, 31-39
Target: 8 minutes

  [opening-frame]
  These are the programs you choose to have... about yourself...
  and who you truly are in Christ.

  [recognition]
  You are a chosen and beloved child of God. Not because of what
  you have done, not because of how you have performed, and not
  because anyone else has decided your worth. You were chosen
  before the foundation of the world. That is who you are. You are
  in charge of knowing and living that truth. What you believe
  about yourself is up to you, and no one else.

  [mirror-check]
  When you look at yourself in the mirror, you see someone who is
  chosen, redeemed, and deeply loved. You see someone who belongs
  to God, who is sealed with His Spirit, and who carries the full
  weight of heaven's approval. That is the person looking back at
  you.

  [not-your-past]
  Your past does not define you. Your failures do not define you.
  The opinions of others do not define you. You are defined by
  what God says — and what God says is that you are His. You are a
  new creation. The old has gone. You walk in the new.

  [known-completely]
  God knows you completely — every thought, every word, every
  hidden thing — and He loves you completely. You are not hidden
  from Him and you are not a disappointment to Him. You are
  fearfully and wonderfully made. That truth does not change based
  on your circumstances or your feelings.

  [tricolon-identity]
  You are chosen.
  You are redeemed.
  You are His.

  [seated-with-christ]
  You are seated with Christ in heavenly places. Not working your
  way there, not striving to earn a seat — already seated. That
  position is yours now. It is not something you achieve; it is
  something you live from.

  [no-condemnation]
  There is no condemnation for you. None. The accuser has no
  standing. Whatever voices have told you that you are too broken,
  too far gone, not enough — those voices do not speak truth. The
  verdict over your life has already been rendered and it reads:
  accepted, adopted, and loved.

  [descriptor-series]
  An accurate description of who you are includes the words:
  chosen... loved... redeemed... adopted... sealed... known...
  accepted... purposed... and fully equipped for everything placed
  before you.

  [nothing-separates]
  Nothing separates you from the love of God. Not trouble. Not
  hardship. Not failure. Not your worst day. Not your longest
  struggle. Not death itself. The love of God in Christ Jesus is
  the fixed point around which everything else in your life
  orbits.

  [his-workmanship]
  You are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works
  which He prepared beforehand. You are not an accident. You are
  not a mistake. You are not a lesser version of someone else. You
  are exactly who He made, placed exactly where He intended, for
  exactly the purpose He designed.

  [tricolon-truth]
  You are not your failures.
  You are not your past.
  You are who God says you are.

  [every-day]
  Every day, you live from who you are — not toward it.
  Every day, you walk in the truth of your identity in Christ.

  [investment]
  You believe in returning to this truth every single day. The
  more you hear it, the more it settles into how you see yourself,
  how you speak about yourself, and how you move through the
  world. Living from your true identity comes naturally,
  confidently, and freely to you.

  [knowing]
  You know who made you. You know who loves you. You know who you
  belong to. And you know that no circumstance, no failure, and no
  voice can take that from you.

  [closing-anchor]
  Those are the programs you choose to have about who you are. You
  already know the truth: you are chosen, redeemed, and deeply
  loved by God... and today is a great day to live like it.
