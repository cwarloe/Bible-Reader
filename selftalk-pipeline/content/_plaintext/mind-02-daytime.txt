======================================================================
MIND & THOUGHT LIFE — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: mind  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: Romans 12:2, Philippians 4:8, 2 Corinthians 10:3-5, Isaiah 26:3, Colossians 3:2
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  My mind is being renewed. I think well.

  [renewed]
  My mind is being transformed by renewal. My thinking is getting
  clearer and more aligned with truth every day.

  [take-captive]
  I take every thought captive and make it obedient to Christ. I
  do not let lies run unchallenged.

  [intentional]
  I am intentional about what I dwell on. I choose what stays in
  my mind and what gets redirected.

  [what-is-true]
  I fix my mind on what is true, honorable, just, pure, lovely,
  and commendable. That is where my attention lives.

  [not-conformed]
  I do not conform to the patterns of the world's thinking. I
  think differently. I see things differently.

  [set-above]
  I set my mind on things above. My perspective is anchored higher
  than my circumstances.

  [perfect-peace]
  I keep my mind stayed on God, and He keeps me in perfect peace.
  Peace follows my focus.

  [strongholds-down]
  I have the power to demolish strongholds — patterns of lying
  thought that have tried to build themselves into my mind. They
  come down.

  [not-my-feelings]
  My feelings are real, but they are not always true. I do not let
  my emotions override what I know to be fact.

  [mind-of-christ]
  I have the mind of Christ. I am learning to live out of it more
  fully every single day.

  [redirect]
  When my mind drifts toward fear, comparison, or self-
  condemnation, I notice it and I redirect. That is the skill I am
  building.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words, I reinforce new thought patterns
  and weaken old ones. My mind is being renewed and truth is
  winning.

  [closing-anchor]
  My mind is being renewed. I think well.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
