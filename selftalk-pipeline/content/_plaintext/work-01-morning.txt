======================================================================
WORK AS HOLY CALLING — MORNING
======================================================================
Suite: work  |  Track: morning  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Ephesians 6:5-9, Colossians 3:23-24, Genesis 1:28, 2:15, Matthew 25:21, Peabody — Secular Work as Full-Time Service
Target: 8 minutes

  [opening-frame]
  These are the programs you choose to have... about yourself...
  and your daily work, your calling, and the holy weight that your
  ordinary efforts carry.

  [recognition]
  You are not an ordinary layman. You are one of the company of
  the saints through whom the wisdom of God is made known in the
  world. Your workplace is not where you go before ministry starts
  — it is where your ministry is. You are in full-time service to
  God, and your assignment is the field you work in every day. You
  are in charge of showing up to that truth. That is up to you,
  and no one else.

  [mirror-check]
  When you look at yourself in the mirror on a Monday morning, you
  see someone on mission. You see someone who patterns their work
  after what they see of God's work — creative, excellent,
  purposeful, and for the good of others. You see someone who
  carries the presence of God into a space that needs it.

  [holy-calling]
  When you do your daily work wholeheartedly, as to the Lord, you
  are carrying out God's original command — to steward and
  cultivate the earth He made. That is not a small thing. That is
  not secular. That is a holy calling that predates the church,
  the law, and everything else. Work itself is sacred, and you
  engage it that way.

  [as-to-the-lord]
  Whatever you do, you do it from the heart — as to the Lord and
  not for human approval. That means your performance review is
  not the final word on your work. The Lord is watching, and He
  sees the sincerity, the integrity, and the quality of what you
  bring. You work for an audience of One.

  [tricolon-work]
  Your work is your worship.
  Your workplace is your mission field.
  Your Monday matters as much as your Sunday.

  [formed-by-hardship]
  You welcome the hardships and disappointments that come through
  your work as sent by God to form Christ within you. You do not
  shun difficulty or resent the hard seasons. You know that God's
  work in you must always precede God's work through you — and the
  workplace is one of His primary classrooms.

  [serving-others]
  As God's light works through you, you serve the people around
  you at work. You love them. You pray for them. You carry
  yourself with integrity before them. You are not merely a
  professional — you are a presence, and that presence carries
  something they cannot name but can feel.

  [descriptor-series]
  An accurate description of who you are at work includes the
  words: excellent... purposeful... servant-hearted...
  trustworthy... in it for more than a paycheck... doing ministry
  in plain clothes... and working with the awareness that your
  ultimate employer sees everything.

  [not-ruled-by-wages]
  Your work is not your god and your paycheck does not define your
  worth. You know how to work hard and how to rest well. You hold
  your resources with an open hand — what you earn is converted
  into kingdom purposes, and you do not expect your full reward in
  this life. You know how to lay up an investment that will
  outlast your career.

  [well-done]
  You are patiently, faithfully working toward the day when you
  will hear the words you most want to hear: "Well done, my good
  and faithful servant. You were faithful with a few things —
  enter into the joy of your Master." That is the reward that
  orients everything else. That is what makes the work worth
  doing.

  [tricolon-mission]
  You are on mission in your daily work.
  You are in full-time service.
  You are making an eternal difference.

  [every-day]
  Every day, you go to work as a person on assignment from God.
  Every day, you bring excellence, integrity, and presence to the
  field He has placed you in.

  [investment]
  You believe in showing up to your work as an act of faithfulness
  to God. Every day of honest, excellent, servant-hearted work
  builds something that matters — in you, in the people around
  you, and in the Kingdom. That investment never goes to waste.

  [knowing]
  You know that your job is not separate from your faith. You know
  that you are a minister wherever you are placed. You know that
  God sees the ordinary work of ordinary people and calls it holy
  — and you know that one day, you will hear Him say it was worth
  it.

  [closing-anchor]
  Those are the programs you choose to have about your work. You
  already know the truth: your work is not secular. It is your
  calling. It is your mission. And it matters eternally... and
  today is a great day to live like it.
