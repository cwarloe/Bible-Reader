======================================================================
PURPOSE & CALLING — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: purpose  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: Ephesians 2:10, Jeremiah 29:11, Romans 8:28, 1 Corinthians 12:7, Colossians 3:23
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  I am here on purpose. My life matters.

  [workmanship]
  I am God's workmanship, created for good works He prepared in
  advance for me to walk in.

  [plans]
  God has plans for me — plans for good and not for harm, a future
  and a hope.

  [all-things]
  God is working all things together for good for me, because I am
  called according to His purpose.

  [gifted]
  I have been given specific gifts for the common good. My gifts
  are real, they are needed, and they are meant to be used.

  [not-accident]
  I am not here by accident. I am in my job, my city, and my
  season on purpose.

  [ordinary-days]
  My ordinary days are where my calling lives. Nothing I do in
  faithfulness is wasted.

  [as-to-the-lord]
  Whatever I do, I do it wholeheartedly — as to the Lord, not just
  for human approval.

  [contribution]
  I have a contribution that only I can make. My voice is needed.
  My presence matters.

  [on-mission]
  I am on mission in my daily life — not waiting for a special
  assignment, but living it now.

  [positioned]
  God positions people for purposes larger than what they can see
  at the time. I am positioned well.

  [not-finished]
  God's purposes for me are not finished. He is not done with me,
  and I am not done with what I was made for.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words, I show up more fully to who I was
  made to be. I live on purpose, with intention, every day.

  [closing-anchor]
  I am here on purpose. My life matters.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SYNTHESIS (second-person coda):
