======================================================================
MIND & THOUGHT LIFE — EVENING
======================================================================
Suite: mind  |  Track: evening  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Psalm 4:4, 19:14, Philippians 4:8, Isaiah 26:3
Target: 5 minutes

  [opening-settle]
  The day of thinking is done. You processed a lot — decisions,
  conversations, worries, replays. It is time to let the mind
  settle. You do not need to keep running the tapes.

  [let-it-go]
  The things you are still turning over from today — you can set
  them down. Your subconscious will work on what matters while you
  sleep. The ones that need your attention will still be there in
  the morning. The ones that do not will fade. You do not have to
  hold them all tonight.

  [search-me]
  Let the words of your mouth and the meditation of your heart be
  acceptable before God tonight. If there is something in your
  thinking that does not belong — a bitterness you have been
  nursing, a fear you have been feeding, a comparison you have
  been making — you can release it now. Hand it over.

  [fixed-for-sleep]
  Fix your last thoughts on what is true. On who God is. On what
  He has promised. On what is good and real and settled. You are
  training your mind even as you fall asleep — so let the last
  signal be truth.

  [he-keeps-you]
  You keep your mind stayed on Him and He keeps you in perfect
  peace. That peace is available to you right now, in the quiet,
  as the day ends. Not because everything is resolved, but because
  He is steady when everything is not.

  [closing-rest]
  Your mind can rest now.
  You have done enough thinking for today.
  He holds what you cannot figure out tonight.
  Let it go. Rest well.
