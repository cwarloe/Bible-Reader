======================================================================
WORK AS HOLY CALLING — EVENING
======================================================================
Suite: work  |  Track: evening  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Colossians 3:23-24, Psalm 127:2, Genesis 2:2-3, Matthew 25:21
Target: 5 minutes

  [opening-settle]
  Think for a moment about the work you did today — whatever it
  looked like. The tasks, the conversations, the problems you
  navigated, the effort you brought. Before you close this day
  out, let it be seen rightly.

  [seen-by-god]
  God saw your work today. Not just the outcomes — He saw the
  integrity behind it, the effort when no one was watching, the
  moments you chose the harder right thing. He is not unimpressed
  by ordinary faithfulness. He values it deeply.

  [as-to-the-lord]
  Whatever you did today, you did it as to the Lord. That is not a
  small thing. That is the lens that makes ordinary work sacred.
  Your daily labor, done with that heart, is worship — and it
  registered today.

  [release-the-incomplete]
  Some things from today are unfinished. Some problems are still
  open. You can release them now. You are not your productivity.
  The work continues tomorrow, and it will be met with fresh
  strength.

  [not-in-vain]
  Nothing done faithfully in the name of the Lord is wasted. The
  quiet work, the unseen effort, the slow accumulation of honest
  days — these build something real in you and in the people
  around you. It counts.

  [rest-is-holy]
  God rested after His work, and He called that rest good.
  You rest now because you worked.
  Tomorrow's assignment will be there.
  Tonight, you lay it down.
  Rest well — you are on mission, and the mission continues in the
  morning.
