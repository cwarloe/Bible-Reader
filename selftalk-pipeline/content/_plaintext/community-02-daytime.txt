======================================================================
COMMUNITY & VULNERABILITY — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: community  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: Ecclesiastes 4:9-12, Hebrews 10:24-25, Galatians 6:2, James 5:16, 1 Corinthians 12:21-22, Romans 15:1
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  I am not alone. I am built for community.

  [designed]
  I am not designed to carry everything by myself. Needing others
  is not a flaw in me — it is how I was made.

  [two-are-better]
  Two are better than one. I am stronger in community than I am in
  isolation. I do not go it alone.

  [bear-burdens]
  I bear the burdens of others, and I allow others to bear mine.
  That is not weakness — it is the fulfillment of the law of
  Christ.

  [confess]
  I confess my struggles to people I trust. I bring things into
  the light rather than carrying them alone in the dark.

  [ask-for-help]
  Asking for help is an act of strength, not weakness. I am secure
  enough to say I need help, and I do.

  [not-isolated]
  I do not isolate. I stay connected. I show up for people, and I
  stay open for them to show up for me.

  [encourage]
  I spur others on toward love and good deeds. I encourage people
  in ways that cost me something, and I receive encouragement in
  return.

  [the-body]
  I am part of a body. I need what others carry, and they need
  what I carry. My connection to others is not optional — it is
  how I function.

  [known]
  I am known by people who care about me. I am not performing for
  them — I let them see me, and I am not afraid of that.

  [real-strength]
  Real strength is not going it alone. Real strength is being
  deeply rooted in relationships that hold when everything else is
  shaking.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words, I reinforce the truth: I am not
  alone, I am built for community, and I am stronger together than
  I am by myself.

  [closing-anchor]
  I am not alone. I am built for community.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
