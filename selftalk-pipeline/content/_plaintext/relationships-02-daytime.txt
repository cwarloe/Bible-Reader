======================================================================
RELATIONSHIPS & LOVE — DAYTIME DRILL
======================================================================
Suite: relationships  |  Track: daytime  |  Perspective: first_person
Sources: 1 Corinthians 13:4-8, Ephesians 4:2-3, 32, Romans 12:9-18, John 15:13
Target: 6 minutes

  [opening-tone]
  I love well. I am not alone.

  [loved-first]
  I am loved by God — patiently, kindly, and without condition.
  That is the foundation I love others from.

  [patient-and-kind]
  I am patient and kind. I do not envy, I do not boast, I am not
  easily offended or self-seeking.

  [no-record]
  I do not keep a record of wrongs. I let go of offenses before
  they become walls.

  [bear-with-others]
  I bear with others in all humility and gentleness. I make every
  effort to keep the bond of peace.

  [forgive]
  I forgive others as God in Christ has forgiven me. Forgiveness
  flows through me because so much has been forgiven in me.

  [sincere]
  My love for others is sincere — not performed, not
  transactional, but genuine and consistent.

  [present]
  I am present with the people God has placed in my life. I show
  up. I listen. I pay attention.

  [lay-down]
  I lay down my life for those I love — in the daily, unglamorous
  acts of choosing people over convenience.

  [not-alone]
  I am not alone. I belong to God first, and through that, to the
  people He has placed in my life.

  [peace]
  As far as it depends on me, I live at peace with everyone. I do
  not carry offense or stir up conflict.

  [invest]
  I invest in relationships. Every act of real love builds
  something that accumulates and lasts.

  [commitment-anchor]
  Each time I hear these words, I reinforce the truth that I am
  loved and that I love others well. That is who I am.

  [closing-anchor]
  I love well. I am not alone.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
