======================================================================
HOPE & REDEMPTION — EVENING
======================================================================
Suite: hope  |  Track: evening  |  Perspective: second_person
Sources: Lamentations 3:21-23, Romans 8:28, Philippians 1:6, Psalm 130:5-7, 1 Peter 1:3
Target: 5 minutes

  [opening-settle]
  Before you close this day, let something settle in you: you are
  not behind. You are not too late. Whatever today looked like —
  however far it was from what you imagined — you are still in the
  story God is writing.

  [mercies-morning]
  His compassions do not fail. They are new every morning. The
  weight of whatever you didn't finish today, whatever you regret,
  whatever you wished had gone differently — it does not carry
  forward into tomorrow as an unpayable debt. Morning brings a
  fresh start. You can rest in that.

  [he-holds-the-future]
  The future you worry about is already in God's hands. He sees
  the end from the beginning, and He is not scrambling. The plans
  He has are not cancelled by the days that didn't go the way you
  hoped. He is not finished with you — not by a long way.

  [living-hope]
  You have been born into a living hope — one that does not depend
  on your circumstances or your track record. It is alive, active,
  and certain. And it belongs to you tonight, whatever this day
  held.

  [wait-for-the-lord]
  Wait for the Lord. His steadfast love is with Him, and with Him
  is full redemption. There are things you cannot yet see that He
  is working on. The story is not over — and the Author of it is
  good.

  [closing-rest]
  Your story is not finished.
  His mercies are new in the morning.
  The work He started in you, He will complete.
  Rest in that tonight.
  You are not behind — you are exactly where the next faithful
  step begins.
